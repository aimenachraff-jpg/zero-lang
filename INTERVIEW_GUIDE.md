# ZERO-LANG — Interview Guide (One-Pager)

**Print or keep open during interview. 45 min + 10 min screen share.**

---

## Pre-Interview (2 min)
- Confirm recording consent (audio only OK)
- "No right answers — I'm learning from you."
- Pseudonym in notes (P1–P8)

---

## 1. Context & Motivation (10 min)

> "Walk me through the **last time you deliberately tried to improve your English at an advanced level**."

**Probe:**
- What triggered it? (exam, job, presentation, frustration, curiosity)
- How long did you stick with it? What made you stop or keep going?
- Describe your **ideal** advanced English learning experience — unconstrained.

## 3. Pain Points & Gaps (10 min)

> "What's the **hardest thing about moving from B2 → C1 → C2**?"

**Probe:** collocations, register, pragmatics, fossilised errors, nuance, speed, confidence

> "When you learn **through content** (science, tech, news), what breaks?"
- Vocabulary overload? Cultural references? Implicit knowledge? Discussion partners?

> "Have you **paid** for any language tool? What would make you pay?"

## 4. ZERO-LANG Concept Test (10 min)

> **"Imagine a FREE, open-source platform that:**
> - **Structured units** (not random chats) where each lesson teaches **advanced English + real world knowledge** (AI, climate, economics, philosophy...)
> - **AI conversation tutor** you can talk/write to about unit topics — **you provide your own API key** (or run local model)
> - **Tracks real progress** against CEFR can-do statements, not streaks
> - No ads, no tracking, no dark patterns, community-built"**

**Ask:**
1. Immediate reaction?
2. Which part most/least valuable?
3. What would make you **come back weekly**?
4. What would make you **contribute** (content, code, review, translation)?

**Notes:**
```

Most valuable: _________________________________________
Least valuable: ________________________________________

Contribution trigger: __________________________________
```

## Tips
- **Listen > talk.** Aim 80/20.
- **Follow the energy.** If they light up on a topic, stay there.
- **"Say more about that"** — best follow-up.
- **Silence is OK.** Wait 3 seconds after they stop.
- **No pitching.** If they ask about ZERO-LANG details: "We're still figuring that out — what would you want?"
