# ZERO-LANG Learner Research — Gate B

## Interview Script (45 min)

### Recruitment Screener (5 min, async)
- CEFR self-assessment: B2, C1, or C2? (or recent test score: IELTS 6.5+, TOEFL 90+, Cambridge First/Advanced/Proficiency)
- Learning goal: work, study, migration, exams, personal growth, other?
- Current tools: What have you used in last 6 months? (ChatGPT, Duolingo, Busuu, ELSA, LingQ, BBC, Coursera, Anki, YouTube, tutors, etc.)
- Willing to share screen for 10 min demo of current workflow?

### Interview Flow

#### 1. Context & Motivation (10 min)
- "Walk me through the last time you deliberately tried to improve your English at an advanced level."
- "What triggered it? (exam, job, presentation, frustration, curiosity)"
- "How long did you stick with it? What made you stop or keep going?"
- "Describe your 'ideal' advanced English learning experience — unconstrained."

#### 2. Current Tool Stack & Workarounds (10 min)
- "Show me how you currently use [ChatGPT / other AI] for English learning." (screen share)
  - What prompts do you use?
  - What's useful? What's annoying?
  - Where does it fail? (hallucination, tone, level, feedback quality)
- "What free resources do you rely on? (podcasts, news, papers, YouTube, textbooks)"
- "How do you track progress? How do you know you're improving?"

#### 3. Pain Points & Gaps (10 min)
- "What's the hardest thing about moving from B2 → C1 → C2?"
  - Probe: collocations, register, pragmatics, fossilised errors, nuance, speed, confidence
- "When you learn *through* content (science, tech, news), what breaks?"
  - Vocabulary overload? Cultural references? Implicit knowledge? Discussion partners?
- "Have you paid for any language tool? What would make you pay?"

#### 4. ZERO-LANG Concept Test (10 min)
> "Imagine a **free, open-source** platform that:
> - Gives you **structured units** (not random chats) where each lesson teaches **advanced English + real world knowledge** (AI, climate, economics, philosophy, etc.)
> - Includes an **AI conversation tutor** you can talk/write to about the unit topics — **you provide your own API key** (or run a local model)
> - Tracks **real progress** against CEFR can-do statements, not streaks
> - No ads, no tracking, no dark patterns, community-built"

- "What's your immediate reaction?"
- "Which part is most/least valuable?"
- "What would make you **come back weekly**?"
- "What would make you **contribute** (content, code, review)?"

#### 5. Rapid Feature Sort (5 min)
Show cards (or read list): "Rank these 1–5 (1 = must have, 5 = don't care):"
- [ ] CEFR-aligned placement test + progress dashboard
- [ ] 20+ units: each = reading + listening + writing + speaking + debate task
- [ ] AI tutor: conversation practice on unit topics (BYOK)
- [ ] AI tutor: writing feedback with error taxonomy (grammar, vocab, register, coherence)
- [ ] Pronunciation practice with accent-neutral intelligibility scoring
- [ ] Spaced repetition for unit vocabulary/collocations
- [ ] Peer discussion forum per unit (async, moderated)
- [ ] Exportable learning portfolio (for employers/universities)
- [ ] Offline-first: download units, sync progress later
- [ ] Interface in your L1 (ES/PT/ZH/AR/FR)
